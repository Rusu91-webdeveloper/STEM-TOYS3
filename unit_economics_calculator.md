#### D. Platform & Operational Costs

- [ ] **Website hosting** (monthly ÷ expected sales)

- [ ] **Customer service costs** (per order)
- [ ] **Legal/compliance costs** (amortized per sale)

---

## 🧮 Unit Economics Formula

### Basic Calculation:

```
Unit Profit = Selling Price - (Product Cost + Fulfillment Cost + Marketing Cost + Operational Cost)
```

### Detailed Breakdown:

```
Revenue per Sale = Selling Price

Costs per Sale:
├── Product Cost (40-50% of selling price)
├── Fulfillment Cost (8-12% of selling price)
├── Marketing Cost (10-20% of selling price)
├── Payment Processing (2-3% of selling price)
└── Operational Overhead (5-10% of selling price)

Net Profit per Sale = Revenue - Total Costs
Profit Margin % = (Net Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
```

## 🎯 Target Metrics for TechTots

### Minimum Viable Metrics:

- **Profit Margin:** At least 15-20%
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Less than 25% of average order value
- **Lifetime Value (LTV):** At least 3x CAC
- **Monthly recurring revenue:** Track subscription products

### Romanian Market Considerations:

- **Average order value:** 150-300 RON
- **Shipping costs:** 15-25 RON within Romania
- **Payment processing:** 2.9% + 0.35 RON (Stripe)
- **VAT:** 19% (built into selling price)

## 🔧 Action Items Based on Analysis:

### If Profit Margins Are Too Low:

1. **Negotiate better supplier prices** (target 10-15% reduction)
2. **Increase selling prices** (market research to justify)
3. **Reduce fulfillment costs** (bulk packaging, efficient shipping)
4. **Optimize marketing spend** (focus on organic traffic)

### If Customer Acquisition Cost Is Too High:

1. **Improve SEO** (long-term organic traffic)
2. **Focus on email marketing** (retention over acquisition)
3. **Partner with influencers** (lower cost than ads)
4. **Create referral program** (customer-driven acquisition)

### If Volume Is Too Low:

1. **Expand product range** (more options = more sales)
2. **Improve conversion rate** (better website UX)
3. **Increase marketing budget** (if CAC is acceptable)
4. **Target new customer segments** (schools, institutions)
