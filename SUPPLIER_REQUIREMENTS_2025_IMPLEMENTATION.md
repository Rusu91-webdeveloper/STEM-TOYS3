### 2. Key Requirements Sections

#### **Eligibility Criteria (8 Criteria)**

1. **Established Business**: Minimum 2 years operations with financial stability
2. **Geographic Location**: EU-based or countries with 7-day EU shipping
3. **Legal Compliance**: Full EU regulations, GDPR, and business law compliance
4. **Quality Standards**: EU safety standards and educational value requirements
5. **Shipping Capabilities**: 7-day maximum delivery to all EU countries
6. **Customer Service**: 24/7 availability with multi-language support
7. **Growth Potential**: Ability to scale operations and meet demand
8. **Innovation Focus**: Commitment to innovative STEM products

#### **Shipping Requirements**

- **European Union**: 3-5 days maximum delivery
- **Non-EU Europe**: 5-7 days maximum delivery
- **International**: 7 days maximum delivery
- Real-time tracking, insurance, and customs handling required

#### **Quality Standards**

- **Safety & Compliance**: CE marking, EN 71 standards, RoHS, REACH
- **Educational Value**: Clear learning objectives, STEM outcomes, teacher
  guides
- **Product Quality**: Premium materials, consistent quality, warranty support
- **Business Standards**: Reliable shipping, 24/7 support, transparent pricing

#### **Compliance Requirements**

- **EU Safety Standards**: CE marking, EN 71, REACH, RoHS, GDPR
- **Business Compliance**: VAT, corporate tax, employment law, environmental
  regulations
- **Quality Assurance**: ISO 9001, regular testing, audits, continuous
  improvement

### 3. Application Process (6 Steps)

1. **Initial Application** (30-45 minutes)
   - Business details, product information, compliance documentation
2. **Documentation Review** (3-5 business days)
   - Legal verification, financial assessment, compliance check
3. **Product Assessment** (5-7 business days)
   - Safety testing, quality evaluation, educational assessment
4. **Shipping & Logistics** (3-5 business days)
   - Shipping capacity, delivery times, tracking systems
5. **Final Decision** (2-3 business days)
   - Executive review, risk assessment, partnership terms
6. **Onboarding & Setup** (1-2 weeks)
   - Account creation, product upload, training sessions

### 4. Commission Structure 2025

- **Standard**: 15% (all approved suppliers)
- **Premium**: 12% (€15k+ monthly sales)
- **Elite**: 10% (€50k+ monthly sales)

Each tier includes enhanced features and support levels.
