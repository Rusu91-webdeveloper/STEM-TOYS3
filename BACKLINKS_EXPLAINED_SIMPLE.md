# 🔗 Backlinks Explained Simply

**What are backlinks and how do you get them?**

---

## 📚 **What is a Backlink?**

### **Simple Definition:**

A **backlink** is when **another website links to your blog**.

**Example:**

Imagine a Romanian parenting blog writes an article about education and includes
this:

```
"Dacă vrei să afli mai multe despre jucării STEM,
citește acest ghid excelent: www.techtots.ro/blog/jucariile-stem-avantaje"
```

That's **1 backlink** to your blog! ✅

---

## 🎯 **Why Do Backlinks Matter?**

### **Think of Backlinks as "Votes":**

**Google's Logic:**

- If 50 websites link to your blog → "This must be good content!"
- If 0 websites link to your blog → "Maybe it's not valuable?"

**It's like social proof for Google:**

- 1 person recommends a restaurant → "Maybe it's okay"
- 100 people recommend a restaurant → "It must be amazing!"

### **Backlinks = Google's #1 Ranking Factor**

**Without backlinks:**

- Even perfect content ranks on page 5-10 ❌

**With backlinks:**

- Same content can rank on page 1! ✅
