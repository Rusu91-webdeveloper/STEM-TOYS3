# Marketing Content Ideas — Q1 2026 (STEM Toys Launch)

Updated: 2026-02-21
Owner: Growth / Content

## Objectives
- Build trust with Romanian parents (practical, non-hype tone)
- Increase organic traffic on high-intent STEM queries
- Create reusable assets for blog + short video + email

## Core Content Pillars
1. **Parent Education** — how to choose age-appropriate STEM toys
2. **Hands-on Experiments** — simple projects children can do at home
3. **Skill Outcomes** — logic, focus, collaboration, confidence
4. **Gift & Seasonal Guides** — birthdays, school milestones, holidays

## 12 High-Impact Content Ideas (Repurpose across channels)

1. **"Cum alegi prima jucărie STEM pe vârste (3-5, 6-8, 9-12)"**
   - Blog + carousel + email mini-guide
   - CTA: category landing pages by age

2. **"Top 10 experimente STEM fără ecran pentru weekend"**
   - Blog + 3 short reels
   - CTA: bundle "Weekend Lab"

3. **"Matematica prin joacă: 5 activități care reduc frustrarea"**
   - Parent pain-point format
   - CTA: math toy collection

4. **"Robotica pentru începători: ce cumperi sub 150 RON"**
   - Price-anchored, conversion-focused
   - CTA: filtered products under 150 RON

5. **"Ce competențe dezvoltă fiecare tip de jucărie STEM"**
   - Visual matrix (toy type → skill)
   - CTA: skill-based shopping

6. **"Kituri STEM pentru frați cu vârste diferite"**
   - Multi-child household angle
   - CTA: family bundles

7. **"Checklist: copilul e pregătit pentru primele exerciții de coding?"**
   - Interactive downloadable PDF
   - CTA: beginner coding kits

8. **"Greșeli frecvente când părinții cumpără jucării educative"**
   - Trust-builder; expert tone
   - CTA: consultation quiz

9. **"30 de minute pe zi: rutina STEM după școală"**
   - Habit-based content
   - CTA: subscription / repeat purchase flow

10. **"Cadouri STEM sub 100 RON care chiar sunt folosite"**
    - Seasonal/high-click format
    - CTA: price-filtered collection

11. **"STEM pentru copii neurodivergenți: ce funcționează în practică"**
    - Inclusive positioning
    - CTA: curated sensory-friendly picks

12. **"Progres în 4 săptămâni: cum măsori învățarea prin joacă"**
    - Outcome storytelling + worksheet
    - CTA: 4-week challenge bundle

## Suggested Weekly Cadence
- 1 long-form blog post
- 2 short social clips
- 1 email newsletter with practical takeaway + product anchor

## Fast A/B Test Angles
- Headline style: practical guide vs transformation promise
- CTA style: "Vezi recomandările" vs "Construiește primul kit"
- Price framing: under 100 RON / 150 RON / premium creator kits

## Notes
- Prioritize credible, parent-useful language over exaggerated clickbait.
- Build every content piece for at least 3-channel reuse (blog, short video, email).
