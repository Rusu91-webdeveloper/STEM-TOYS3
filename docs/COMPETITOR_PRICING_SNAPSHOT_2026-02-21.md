# Competitor Pricing Snapshot — Romania STEM Toys

Date: 2026-02-21
Scope: Quick benchmark for launch pricing decisions

## Data Collection Notes
- Attempted multi-source search via `web_search` (Brave API), but API key is not configured in this environment.

- Other stores tested were blocked or returned non-usable pages during this work block.

## Observed Price Bands (eMAG STEM listing snapshot)

### Entry Level (Impulse/Addon)
- **~26–65 RON**
- Typical products: small math games, balancing toys, compact educational kits

### Core Mid-Tier (Primary conversion zone)
- **~70–130 RON**
- Most frequently observed range in listing
- Typical products: classroom-style STEM packs, logic sets, beginner robotics/math kits

### Premium (Gift-focused / larger kits)
- **~150–205 RON**
- Typical products: larger branded or multi-piece kits

### High-End Ceiling (less frequent)
- **~289–305 RON**
- Seen for larger or branded premium items

## Launch Pricing Recommendations (TechTots)

1. **Anchor most hero SKUs in 89–149 RON**
   - Aligns with observed mid-tier demand while preserving room for promotions.

2. **Maintain an entry ladder at 49–79 RON**
   - Supports first-purchase conversion and bundle upsell.

3. **Use premium tier at 169–249 RON with value framing**
   - Add stronger differentiators (learning outcomes, age maps, gift packaging).

4. **Bundle strategy**
   - "Starter bundle" target: **129–159 RON**
   - "Family/advanced bundle" target: **199–279 RON**
