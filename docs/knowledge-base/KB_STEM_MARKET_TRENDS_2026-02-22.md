# KB: STEM Toys Market Trends (2026-02-22)

## Core Insight
STEM toys category is growing, but published market size varies heavily by source scope. Strategy should be built on ranges and demand drivers, not a single TAM figure.

## Signals
- GMI (Dec 2025): growth toward ~$13.8B by 2034, CAGR ~8.8%.
- Grand View: growth toward ~$1.83B by 2030, CAGR ~7.2%.
- Common directional themes: STEAM expansion, hybrid physical+digital products, sustainability as purchase signal.

## Actionable Rules
1. Use low/base/high market scenarios in planning decks.
2. Merchandise by learning outcomes (critical thinking, creativity, engineering reasoning).
3. On PDPs, include skill outcomes + age fit + safety notes.

## Watchlist
- Certification standards and safety compliance communication.
- Parent demand for structured learning journeys.
- Subscription fatigue vs subscription value perception.
