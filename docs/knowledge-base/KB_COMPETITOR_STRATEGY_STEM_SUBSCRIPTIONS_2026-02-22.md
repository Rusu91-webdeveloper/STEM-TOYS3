# KB: Competitor Strategy Patterns — STEM Subscription Brands (2026-02-22)

## Observed Patterns
### CrunchLabs
- Strong founder-led brand authority and engineering identity.
- Clear progression narrative (build → hack → skills growth).
- Social proof and gifting pathways embedded in conversion flow.

### MEL Science
- Subscription recurring model with monthly delivery.
- Physical kit + app/VR/AR blended experience.
- Safety/compliance communication used as major trust lever.

### KiwiCo (limited scrape in this session)
- Multi-line crate architecture and age-segmented positioning.

## Strategic Takeaways for TechTots
1. Compete on **learning journey clarity**, not only SKU count.
2. Use structured age/skill progression in merchandising.
3. Add explicit safety + educational outcome framing on each product page.
4. Test phased recurring offer after one-off + bundle model proves conversion.

## Suggested Experiments
- A/B test PDP modules: “What your child learns” vs generic descriptions.
- Test gift-focused landing vs skill-progression landing.
- Pilot 30-day STEM challenge email/on-site journey for retention.
