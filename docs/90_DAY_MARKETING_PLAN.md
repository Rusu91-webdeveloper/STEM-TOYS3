# 90-Day Marketing Execution Plan

## Overview
Comprehensive marketing plan for TechTots.ro - 4 hours/day, beginner-friendly execution.

**Goal:** Build brand awareness, drive traffic, increase conversions, and establish TechTots as the go-to STEM toys retailer in Romania.

---

## Weekly Time Allocation (4 hours/day = 20 hours/week)

- **Content Creation (Videos):** 10 hours/week (2 hours/day)
- **SEO Content (Blog Pages):** 4 hours/week (2 pages/week)
- **Email Marketing:** 3 hours/week
- **Social Media Engagement:** 2 hours/week
- **Analytics & Optimization:** 1 hour/week

## Content Calendar Template

### Daily Video Schedule
- **Monday:** Educational/How-to content
- **Tuesday:** Product reviews/unboxing
- **Wednesday:** Parent testimonials/stories
- **Thursday:** Comparison/versus content
- **Friday:** Weekend project ideas
- **Saturday:** Community highlights
- **Sunday:** Weekly wrap-up/planning

### Weekly SEO Content
- **Monday:** Research & outline (1 hour)
- **Tuesday:** Write first draft (1 hour)
- **Wednesday:** Edit & optimize (1 hour)
- **Thursday:** Publish & promote (1 hour)

## Email Capture Flows

### 1. Welcome Flow (3 emails)
- **Email 1:** Welcome + 10% discount code
- **Email 2:** Educational content + product recommendations
- **Email 3:** First purchase incentive

### 2. Abandoned Cart Flow (3 emails)
- **Email 1:** Reminder (2 hours after abandonment)
- **Email 2:** Urgency (24 hours after)
- **Email 3:** Final offer (48 hours after)

### 3. Post-Purchase Flow (2 emails)
- **Email 1:** Thank you + usage tips
- **Email 2:** Related products + review request
